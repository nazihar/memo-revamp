# External penetration test — scope

> One external penetration test covers Phases 0–4 and Phase 1b. It runs after Phase 1b, before milestone M1 (Phase 4 Q2, 2026-11-18; Phase 3 Q4). Phase 3 E07 books the tester (Phase 3 Q5, open) and runs the test with its retest. Each phase adds its part of the scope here when it closes. Phase 1b E10 adds federated sign-in, passkeys, the token issuer, key management and break-glass. Phase 3 E07 adds the Phase 0–3 surfaces, using `asvs-checklist.md` as the inventory. Findings are mapped to rows of `asvs-checklist.md`. Every critical and high finding is fixed and retested before M1 (`TESTING_STRATEGY.md` §6).

## Phase 4: forums, meetings, minutes, resolutions and actions (written at phase close, 2026-11-28)

### What the tester gets
- **A test installation** built from the release images (Helm or the on-prem Compose profile). Use a copy of dev, never dev itself, because dev keeps demo data that later journeys depend on. Use `MEMO_DEMO_SEED=true` without the `prod` profile, so that the demo tenant and its committee cycle are seeded (runbook `committees.md` and the E05–E07 seeds).
- **A second tenant** with its own administrator, to test isolation between tenants.
- **Demo accounts** in the roles that matter for Phase 4. The owner hands over the passwords and TOTP secrets out of band; they are never in this file.

  | Account | Role in the demo cycle |

- **The OpenAPI contract** (`contracts/openapi.json`), the authorisation matrices (`backend/src/test/resources/authz/p4-*.yaml`), the leak-suite registry (`PaperSurfaceRegistry`), the threat models (`docs/plan/phase-4-forums-meetings/epic-0*/threat-model.md`) and the runbooks (`committees.md`, `meetings-and-agendas.md`, `meeting-invitations.md`, `minutes-and-resolutions.md`, `action-items.md`, and `decision-register-and-analytics.md` once E08 T10 has written it). This is a white-box test.

### The claim under test
A person never learns anything about an agenda item they may not read: not its title, papers, discussion, decision text, vote, dissent, actions or counts. This holds whether they are outside the item's restriction, above their clearance, a former member, a guest, or a director recused by their own declaration (recused, or waiting for a ruling). They learn nothing through any endpoint, file, email, calendar file, notification, export, count, facet, ranking or error (overview risk R1). The in-house evidence is the canary and differential leak suites (`PaperLeakIT`, `RecusalLeakIT`, `RegisterLeakIT`, `AnalyticsLeakIT`) and the meta-test that every controller of the Phase 4 modules is registered. The tester's job is to find a path these suites do not have.

### Known and accepted (not findings unless the tester shows more)
- A secretary can type an item's discussion outside its block, or into a decision's text or an action's context. The leak check refuses a copy that contains a redacted item's recorded text, but it cannot recognise free text typed elsewhere. The runbooks cover this (E06, E07 and E08 residual risks).
- Restricted lines show that a numbered decision exists at a meeting. The same viewers already see this on the agenda (E08).
- iMIP is unsigned (RFC 6047 §2.2). Anyone who learns a meeting's random UID could forge an update. S/MIME is a follow-up (E05).
- Forwarded invitations carry the join link (E05 I3).
- Directors can print or photograph papers. Watermarking and the secure viewer come in Phase 5.
- A recused member who reads the memo through the memo's own rule can still read it. The meeting register shows this (E04 I6).

### Not in this test
- Mobile apps and the token issuer's clients (Phase 6a; the issuer itself is in 1b's part).
- Board books, voting and signatures (Phase 5; their own pen test before M3, `TESTING_STRATEGY.md` §6).
- The recorded run in real calendar clients (E05 T9). It is carried for the owner and is not a security test.
