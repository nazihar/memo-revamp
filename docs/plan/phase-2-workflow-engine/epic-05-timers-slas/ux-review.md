# E05 — UX review

The per-epic gate is delegated for Phase 2 (overview §10, Q1). E05's screens follow E03 and E04 patterns; findings from the next demo session are recorded here.

## Flows
1. **Business calendar (admin):** Administration → Business calendar → weekly hours per weekday (Sunday–Thursday 9:00–17:00 by default; several periods a day allowed) and time zone → Special days: add a holiday, a range (Eid: four days closed), an extra working Saturday, or other hours for a period (Ramadan 9:30–14:30) → Preview: "a 2-day SLA started now is due on Tuesday 12 May, 15:00".
2. **Due and overdue (approver):** the inbox shows "Due today 15:00" or "Overdue by 1 working day" and can sort by due time; the memo view shows the same under the current step.
3. **Reminder:** "Reminder: M-2026-00042 is due in 4 hours" in-app and by email (reference only).
4. **Escalation:** the holder's supervisor gets "M-2026-00042 was escalated to you"; the history shows "Escalated by the workflow: overdue by 1 day".
