- **T5 (simulation API):** `POST …/{key}/versions/{n}/simulate` runs a stored version, or an unsaved body, through the pure engine in memory (`DefinitionSimulator`).
  - It uses the organisation's resolvers, substitutes, settings and forward limits, and the DoA in force matched on the sample (`DoaMatcher.matchAt`).
  - Eligibility comes from `WorkflowSubject.ineligibleAt`. For memos, that is an active account and clearance for the sample classification.
  - Every open decision gets its positive action from its holder until the instance ends, a task is blocked, or 500 commands.
  - Made-up ids carry a fixed high half, so they can never be mistaken for rows.
  - The answer: steps in order, each with its outcome, people (names, acting-for), blocked reason, the branch taken (index, condition, next), DoA group and the SLA as written; plus `ended`.
  - Invalid bodies get 422 `DEFINITION_INVALID` with the problems. `RateLimit.WORKFLOW_SIMULATION_PER_USER` allows 30 a minute.
  - Tests: `DefinitionSimulationIT` covers:
    - both branches;
    - parallel all;
    - notify recipients;
    - an uncleared committee member blocking the step;
    - 422;
    - no instance, task or audit row written;
    - the 31st call in a minute refused.
  - Matrix: `p2-e06-builder.yaml`.
  - The unit test `DefinitionSimulatorTest` named in the test plan was not written. DoA and queue steps in a simulation are exercised by the exit journey (T9) instead.
- **T1 (diagram and Steps view, read-only):** the version page has three tabs: **Diagram**, **Steps** and **JSON**. Versions in review and published versions open on the diagram. Drafts open on JSON until editing arrives (T2).
  - **Diagram:** React Flow 12.12.1 with dagre 3.1.3, top-down; positions are computed and never stored.
    - Start, every step and End are focusable nodes, named for screen readers ("Step 1 of 5, Review, Credit review. The holder of Head of Credit Leads to By classification.").
    - React Flow's own strings are localised through `ariaLabelConfig`.
    - Nodes are not draggable.
  - **Steps view:** an ordered list with a heading per step; who it goes to, the deadline in words, where it leads (branch conditions and otherwise), and a problem count from the JSON pointers.
  - **Backend:** `VersionResponse.labels` names the people, positions, units and roles a body mentions. It uses `UserSummaries`, `OrgDirectory.positions`, a new `OrgNames.units` and `RoleHolders.names`.
  - **Lint:** a `no-restricted-syntax` rule forbids `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` (threat E1).
  - **Bundle:** React Flow and dagre load only with the workflow definition page (82 kB gzip in that route's chunk, plus 15 kB of CSS as a file). Nothing injects inline styles, so it runs under `style-src 'self'`; the E2E on the real stack confirms it.
  - **Tests:**
    - `model.test.ts`: graph shape, dangling edges, top-down layout, 100 steps, and fast-check properties (one edge per connection, the body untouched, every node placed);
    - `describe.test.ts`: durations, assignees, deadlines and targets in words;
    - `views.test.tsx`: the Steps view and the diagram, with axe;
    - `workflows.test.tsx`: the review screen across the three tabs;
    - E2E: the published "Credit facility" draws, names its first step, shows its deadline in the Steps view, and passes axe;
    - `DefinitionSimulationIT`: labels.
  - **jsdom stubs** for React Flow (`src/test/react-flow.ts`) are installed per test file, so other tests keep jsdom's real element sizes.
  - **Not done:** an Arabic render test of the builder. Admin catalogue parts load lazily in component tests; `i18n:verify` checks the Arabic keys instead.
- **T2 (editing core):** a draft is edited in the builder.
  - **Commands** (`edit.ts`, pure): add a step after another or at the start (it takes over where that step led; after a branch, its otherwise); connect any slot (start, next, a condition, otherwise); remove a step (what led to it is re-pointed when it led to one place, otherwise left for validation); set properties (an undefined value removes the key); add and remove conditions.
    - New steps get unused ids from their type and the minimum their type needs.
    - **Undo and redo** keep up to 100 bodies. Typing in one field extends one undo step instead of one per key, and JSON-view edits count as one change.
  - **Panels:**
    - The **Steps view** has "Add a step at the start" and a **Change** button per step that opens its panel inline.
    - In the **diagram**, selecting a step (click, or Enter on a focused node) opens the same panel beside the diagram, never over it.
    - The panel has the name, Leads to, a branch's conditions (raw CEL until T4) with their targets and otherwise, "Add a step after this one" (the palette: every type but `sign`) and **Remove** (confirmed).
  - **Live validation:** half a second after a change, the unsaved body goes to `validate`; only the newest answer counts. A polite status line says "Checking…" or how many problems were found, and the problems badge their steps.
  - **Save** keeps If-Match. On 412 the local work stays, with **Reload the saved version** or **Copy my changes as JSON**. Leaving with unsaved changes asks first (`UnsavedChangesGuard`). Drafts now open on the diagram too.
  - **Tests:**
    - `edit.test.ts`: every command; history limits. Properties: any edits, all undone, give back the start; ids stay unique; nothing outside `steps` changes.
    - `workflows.test.tsx`: add a step from the Steps view, the debounced check and its badge, undo and redo, save with If-Match, and a 412 keeping the work.
    - `views.test.tsx`: selecting a diagram step opens its panel; renaming and connecting send their commands.
  - **Size:** the Arabic admin catalogue part is now 11.2 of its 15 KiB limit. T3 and T4 add strings; if it nears the limit, the builder's strings get their own part.
- **T3 (panels: who and what):**
  - **Catalogue:** `GET /api/v1/admin/workflow-builder/catalogue`, for authors and reviewers.
    - It returns the attributes per item type, the tenant's roles and queues, published fragments only, and the step types without `sign`.
    - It moved off `/workflow-definitions/catalogue`, which a definition keyed `catalogue` would have shadowed. The design is updated.
  - **Assignee panel:** every kind.
    - People and positions come from the existing directory picker. Units come from the directory; roles and queues from the catalogue.
    - The supervisor's levels and a unit head's units-up are selects. Number fields clamped as you typed and made "2" hard to enter.
    - The initiator is offered only where allowed (notices, T4).
  - **Actions:** the positive action is fixed by the type, and a review cannot reject. Per action, "needs a remark". A forward limit appears when Forward is offered, and is cleared with it.
  - **Parallel:** the mode (all, any, a number), n, and 2–20 people or groups, each with the assignee panel.
  - **Other types:** the sub-workflow picks a published fragment; the forum key is a text field; DoA and route steps explain where they go.
  - Names picked in a panel show in the views before the draft is saved.
  - **Tests:**
    - `BuilderCatalogueIT`: attributes, roles, a queue, a published fragment but not a draft one, no `sign`. Matrix row added.
    - `panels.test.tsx`: a role in a unit, a queue, the supervisor's levels and a position through the picker each write the right JSON; actions, remarks and the forward limit; a parallel step's mode and people; axe.

- **T4 (panels: when and where):**
  - **Conditions** (`conditions.ts`, pure): rows of attribute · comparison · value joined by "all of" (`&&`) or "any of" (`||`) ↔ CEL.
    - Comparisons follow the type: numbers compare; strings are or are not, or are one of a list; booleans are yes or no. Timestamps are CEL only.
    - Doubles always print with a decimal point, or CEL would refuse the comparison.
    - Anything else (parentheses, mixed joins, functions, attribute against attribute) reads as undefined, opens as CEL and is never rewritten.
    - A new command `setCondition` sets a branch's condition and declares the attributes it reads in the same undo step: conditions compile against the declared attributes (ADR-0039).
    - Typing in one condition is one undo step.
  - **Deadline panel** on every human step but `sign`: due after an amount and a unit (anything combining units shows as ISO text), working time (on by default), a reminder before, up to three after, and what happens when late. A reassignment names its target with the assignee panel; the initiator is never offered.
  - **Notify panel:** the people or groups to tell (the initiator allowed) and the message key.
  - The value of a condition row keeps what is typed, so a number can be cleared and retyped. This was found by the test: a controlled number field turned "3" into "23".
  - **Tests:**
    - `conditions.test.ts`: reading, the CEL kept as written, printing, and a property that rows written as CEL read back as the same rows;
    - `edit.test.ts`: `setCondition` declares once;
    - `panels-when.test.tsx`: a simple condition edited and joined, an unreadable one left alone, every deadline control, notices; axe.
  - **Size:** the Arabic admin catalogue part is now 12.6 of 15 KiB. A path loads one part, so a separate builder part needs the loader to take two. Do that if T6–T8 bring it near the limit.
- **T6 (simulation UI):** a **Simulate** section under the views.
  - **Inputs:** who starts it (the people picker); a sample value for each attribute the definition declares (classification rank as a number; memo type from the memo types; yes/no for booleans); and, when the definition has a route step, the approvers the initiator would choose.
  - **What it sends:** the body on screen, saved or not, to `simulate`.
  - **Result:** a polite status line says whether it would be approved, stop and wait for an administrator, or hit the step limit. An ordered list shows each step reached: who it reaches (with "for" a substitute's principal), why it would stop (not cleared, inactive, nobody found), which condition held or "otherwise", the DoA group, and the deadline in words.
  - **Path marking:** the steps reached are marked in the diagram (a ring and an icon with a name, not colour alone, plus "Reached in the simulation" in the node's accessible name) and in the Steps view (a badge). The result is dropped as soon as the body changes.
  - **Errors:** 422 says the workflow has problems; 429 says to try again in a minute; anything else says it could not run.
  - **Catalogue split:** the builder's messages now have their own catalogue part, `builder` (ids `builder.*`, loaded with `/admin/workflows` pages).
    - The shell's `CatalogueGate` takes every part a path needs (`partsForPath`).
    - This answers the T4 note. The Arabic admin part drops to 9.5 KiB; the builder part is 4.2 KiB, both of 15.
  - **Tests:**
    - `workflows.test.tsx`: simulate from the page (the initiator required first, the request carrying the sample, the path with its condition, block reason and deadline, the Steps view marking both steps), and the Arabic definition page loading both parts;
    - `simulation.test.tsx`: the route inputs, 422 and 429, axe.
  - The full web suite passes apart from four files that time out under load and pass when rerun (the known flake).
- **T7 (diff and review):**
  - **The diff** (`diff.ts`, pure) matches steps by id. Added steps come in the newer version's order, removed ones after.
    - A changed step lists the property paths that differ. Deadlines are compared one level down (`sla.due`, `sla.remind.before`) and conditions per branch (`branches[1].when`), so a change says which part moved.
    - Key order is ignored.
  - **In words** (`describe-change.ts`): what it was is named from the older version (a removed target still has its name), what it is from the newer one. People, positions, units and roles are by name, durations and deadlines in words, actions by their labels.
  - **The Changes tab:**
    - It appears when there is a version to compare with: the published one, or else the one before.
    - A reviewer of a version in review opens on it. "Compared with" picks any other version.
    - It shows a summary line, the diagram with added and changed steps marked (a dashed border and a word badge, plus the accessible name; never colour alone), changes to the workflow as a whole, and a list step by step, removed steps included.
  - **Tests:**
    - `diff.test.ts`: added, removed, changed; the moved part of a deadline; key order. Properties: a version against itself is empty; each step appears at most once, and only if it differs.
    - `changes.test.tsx`: the summary, the list in words (a person's name before and after, "Tell the author → Board", "none → Due in 2 working days"), the diagram's marks, comparing with another version; axe.
    - `workflows.test.tsx`: the reviewer lands on Changes.
