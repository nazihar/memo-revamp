# Minutes and resolutions

**When:** a secretary records the committee's decisions, makes, circulates or corrects the minutes, or issues a certified extract; or someone asks "why didn't this memo move on?", "why is the resolution number missing?", "why can't I send the minutes to the chair?", "why does this director's copy leave an item out?" or "is this extract genuine?". Background: ADR-0065 (decisions and resolutions), ADR-0066 (minutes: drafting, the approval path, the record), ADR-0051 (authenticated approvals), ADR-0062 (recusal), ADR-0064 (quorum and its override), Phase 4 E06 `design.md`. Meetings, agendas, attendance and declarations are in [meetings-and-agendas.md](meetings-and-agendas.md); committee types in [committees.md](committees.md); the memo's own final record in [final-records.md](final-records.md).

## Who does what

| Who                                                                                                            | Can                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The committee's secretaries in office, and holders of `forums.manage`                                          | Record and correct decisions, keep notes for the minutes, decide a consent section, mark the meeting as held; make the draft, edit it, send it to the chair, circulate it, answer comments, end the comment period, take it back to the draft; propose corrections; set a committee's starting resolution number (`forums.manage`, step-up) |
| A secretary who declared an interest in an item                                                                | Nothing on that item (403 `RECUSED`), and nothing on the minutes as a whole (the draft holds every item): another secretary does it                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| The chair in office (the vice-chair when the chair declared an interest in any minuted item, or there is none) | Reviews the draft (suggested changes in review mode, comments, agree or send back); approves the minutes with a fresh step-up; approves or declines corrections                                                                                                                                                                             |
| The committee's secretaries in office (not `forums.manage`)                                                    | Issue certified extracts of a resolution (step-up)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Members, alternates who attended, people asked for comments                                                    | Read their own copy of the circulated minutes; "No comments" or a suggestion; after approval, their own copy of the record                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `forum.read` holders (for example the auditor)                                                                 | The resolution register and the approved records they may see; no draft, no notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| The memo's readers                                                                                             | Their memo's resolution and its extracts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

Everyone else gets 404. A recused reader sees an item's heading, "You declared an interest in this item and took no part" and the decision's outcome, number, text and conditions, never its vote, names, notes or discussion.

## Decisions and resolution numbers

- **Recording:** from the meeting's start until the minutes are approved, in the meeting's Decisions tab. Each decision is checked against the quorum **at the time it was taken** (a snapshot is stored with it): only people present in a voting seat and not recused can be named as dissenting or abstaining, and the counts cannot exceed them. "Took no part" is derived, never typed.
- **Not quorate:** refused (409 `NOT_QUORATE`) unless the committee type allows the override with a reason (`meetings.quorumOverride` WITH_REASON). The decision is then marked "To be ratified"; a later quorate decision of the committee that approves or notes it ratifies it. The chair and the other secretaries are told.
- **Consent section:** decided in one action; items lifted for discussion and items already decided are left out. Each item gets its own decision (approved or noted by its purpose).
- **Numbers** (`BRC/2026/014` by default, per committee and year, from the committee type's format): given when the meeting is marked as held, in agenda order, by the `minutes-release` job; a decision recorded after that is numbered at once. Numbers are gapless and never reused: correcting a resolution into something that is not one **voids** its number with the reason, and the next decision takes the next number. The register's **Check** lists missing, duplicated or stray numbers (it should always say "complete").
- **Starting number:** a committee moving onto the system mid-year can start at its next paper number, once per period and before any number is given (`forums.manage`, step-up; 409 `ALREADY_STARTED` afterwards).
- **Corrections** before approval: a new entry with a reason; the old one stays in the history. Once the memo has moved on, the outcome cannot change (409 `RELEASED`): correct the wording only, and ask the author to resubmit if the outcome was wrong. Once the minutes are approved, nothing changes (409 `FROZEN`): use a correction to the minutes.

## Release: the way back to the memo

- **When:** at the meeting's end by default (`memoResumes` AT_MEETING_END), or when the minutes are approved (ON_MINUTES_APPROVAL), per committee type. The policy is copied onto the minutes when the meeting is held.
- **What:** each decided submission item moves its memo on with the outcome; the note sheet gets "Management Credit Committee — Approved with conditions — Resolution MCC/2026/001" with the text and conditions, and the memo's final record lists it with the approvals. Deferred items go back to the committee's tray with the target; referred items open a linked submission at the other committee.
- **Failures:** one failing decision never holds the others back. The job tries five times in all (backoff from 10 seconds); after the last try the failure is audited (`minutes.decision.release_failed`) and the secretaries get `DECISIONS_RELEASE_FAILED`.

  | Cause                                                                                                    | What happens, what to do                                                                                                                             |
  | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | The submission was withdrawn or closed meanwhile (no longer queued or accepted)                          | Skipped, not a failure (`NOT_WAITING`, a warning in the log): the decision stays recorded and nothing moves                                          |
  | The submission still waits but the memo's workflow refuses (moved on by other means, a step changed)     | Fails after the tries: tell the author and the workflow administrator; the decision stays recorded, and the minutes say what the committee decided   |
  | The job acts as the decision's recorder (the workflow needs a signed-in actor); their account cannot act | Fails: have another secretary correct the decision (same outcome, a reason), which releases it at once as them                                       |
  | A database or workflow outage                                                                            | Retried with backoff; nothing to do if it recovers within the retries                                                                                |

  The audit's `minutes.decision.released` for each memo confirms the release. No screen retries a failed release (known limit).

## Templates and content controls

- **The template** is per committee type (Minutes tab): the built-in one (English; an Arabic one is shipped) or an uploaded .docx, checked on upload with the minutes vocabulary. Problems name the tag and suggest the right one (`{{item.discusion}}` → "Did you mean `{{item.notes}}`?"); `minutes.status`, `minutes.approvedOn` and `minutes.approvedBy` are **deprecated** (a warning: the approval is on the record's cover and stamp, never in the document). Preview fills it with a sample meeting. A type with template problems cannot be submitted.
- **The block rule:** `{{?items}}` and `{{/items}}` must each be a paragraph of their own in the body (not in a table, text box or header): 422 `BLOCK_PLACEMENT` otherwise.
- **Content controls:** the draft wraps each minuted item in a locked content control tagged `memo-item:<id>`. Copies for readers are made by replacing whole controls, so a recused director's copy never holds a word of that item. Rows of meeting-level tables are tagged `memo-row:<id>` and dropped from the copies that redact their item.
- **The structure check** runs on every saved version. A missing, duplicated, nested, unlocked or unknown block stops sending, circulating and approving (409 `STRUCTURE` with the items). **"Restore item n"** puts a missing block back after the previous one; "Fill an item again" refills one block from the decisions and notes; "Make the whole draft again" replaces every edit (`replaceEdits`).
- **Leak check:** before any copy is converted, the job looks for every recorded text of the items it redacts (notes, dissent reasons, papers, actions, decision text where the reader may not see it) anywhere in the document. If one is found (for example a secretary typed it outside its block), the copy fails `LEAK` and is not served. Find the text with "See the copy for…", move it into its item's block, save.

## Making and approving the minutes

- **Draft:** from the held meeting, by a secretary not recused from any item. The `minutes-draft` job fills the template (attendance, apologies, declarations, decisions with votes and dissent, notes, actions). Edit it in the browser (ONLYOFFICE, several secretaries at once). Refused while someone has it open (`BEING_EDITED`), while it is being made (`GENERATING`), when a draft exists and "Make the whole draft again" was not chosen (`DRAFT_EXISTS`), before the meeting is held (`WRONG_STATE`) or after approval (`FROZEN`). A submission item without a decision (`UNDECIDED_ITEMS`) does not stop the draft; it stops sending to the chair, circulating and confirming.
- **Chair's review:** "Send to the chair" chooses the reviewer then. The chair suggests changes in review mode (tracked) or sends it back with a comment; a secretary can record an agreement given by phone or email ("how"). Suggested changes left in the document stop circulation (`UNRESOLVED_CHANGES`): accept or reject them, then "Circulate for comments". Nobody able to review (`NO_REVIEWER`): circulate with a reason.
- **Circulation:** each reader gets a PDF copy for their own redaction set, stamped DRAFT (the `minutes-copies` job; at most 50 distinct sets, else `TOO_MANY_COPIES`). Comments are "No comments" or a suggestion on an item or in general; the secretary adopts or not, with a note. The deadline is `circulationWorkingDays` working days on the business calendar (changeable). A save while circulated rebuilds the copies two minutes later.
- **The two approval modes** (committee type):

  | Mode                                                                | Path                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Who approves                      |
  | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
  | At the next meeting (`AT_NEXT_MEETING`, Board and board committees) | Circulated minutes are linked to the next meeting's "Minutes of the last meeting" item when its agenda is published (or at once if already published). A decision "Approved" on that item sends them to the chair; "Deferred" leaves them circulated | The chair confirms with a step-up |
  | By circulation (`BY_CIRCULATION`, management committees)            | The comment period ends when every member has answered, when the deadline passes (the sweep) or early with a reason                                                                                                                                  | The chair confirms with a step-up |

- **Confirmation** (one transaction): every decision becomes final, the meeting becomes "Minutes approved" (attendance and declarations freeze), and an approval row is written with how the chair authenticated (ADR-0051), the .docx's SHA-256 and a hash chained to the committee's previous approval. ON_MINUTES_APPROVAL releases now. Refusals: 403 `NOT_CONFIRMER` (only the chair in office, never on their behalf), the step-up problem, `STRUCTURE` if a newer version was saved or a block is damaged, and the checks of circulation (`NO_DRAFT`, `UNDECIDED_ITEMS`, `UNRESOLVED_CHANGES`).
- **Reminders** (the hourly meeting sweep's last step): comments closing (from 09:00 on the deadline's day), draft overdue (`draftWorkingDays` after the meeting), three working days with the chair. Each once. Days, deadlines and 09:00 are the **tenant's** (`default.timezone`, the business calendar), not the meeting's zone; the demo tenant is on Asia/Dhaka.

## The record and fixity

- The confirmation asks the `minutes-record` job for **record version 1**: a cover (committee, meeting, how and by whom approved, the factors, the hashes, the previous approval), the approved .docx as PDF/A, the resolutions annex, and a stamp on every page; validated by veraPDF (PDF/A-2u), stored object-locked for the committee type's retention (`minutes.record`), audited with its SHA-256. Each reader with a non-empty redaction set gets an **approved copy** (`minutes.approved-copy`), locked the same way.
- **What must be running:** the scheduler, ONLYOFFICE (`documents` profile; conversion to PDF and PDF/A), the scanner and object storage. Without a document server configured at all, copies and records fail at once (`NO_CONVERTER`).
- **Conversion retries (since #255):** every conversion is sent to ONLYOFFICE under a key of its own (the caller's label plus a random suffix), so a retry is a new conversion and never gets back a failure ONLYOFFICE kept (before, one transient `-3` failed a copy or record for good). A transient error (unreachable, a timeout, `-1`, `-3`, `-4`, `-6`) is retried by the job; `-2`, `-5`, `-7` to `-10` are final.
- **How many tries** (backoff from 10 seconds, plus each try's own time: 10 s to connect, up to 150 s of polling): `minutes-draft` and `minutes-copies` (draft and approved copies) 3; `minutes-record`, `minutes-extract` and `minutes-release` 5. Readers meanwhile get "being prepared" (202 or 409 `RECORD_PENDING`).
- **After the last try** the row is FAILED, with `ERROR` for a draft copy and `CONVERTER_<code>` for a record, an approved copy or an extract (`CONVERTER_UNREACHABLE`, `CONVERTER_SLOW`, `CONVERTER_HTTP_nnn`, `CONVERTER_CONVERTER_M3`), audited `minutes.copy.failed` or `minutes.record.failed`. **Nothing asks again by itself:**
  - a draft copy: the next save while circulated makes new copies two minutes later;
  - an extract: issue it again (409 `EXTRACT_FAILED` says so);
  - **a record or an approved copy stays FAILED** (readers keep getting 409 `RECORD_PENDING` or 202), and no screen or job retries it (known limit). Bring ONLYOFFICE back, then raise it with engineering: the record must be asked again for that version. Do not change the rows by hand.
- **Check** on the approved page re-hashes the approval chain, the approved .docx and the reader's file from storage: `DOCUMENT_CHANGED`, `DOCUMENT_MISSING`, `FILE_CHANGED`, `FILE_MISSING` are incidents (object lock should make them impossible; see final-records.md).
- **Fixity:** the weekly memo fixity run also checks the minutes' records, approved copies and extracts (`minutes.record.verified`/`.mismatch`, `minutes.copy.verified`/`.mismatch`, `minutes.extract.verified`/`.mismatch`). A mismatch is an incident: keep the evidence, compare with the audit chain.

## Corrections after approval

- Approved minutes are never edited. A secretary not recused from the item **proposes** a correction (an item or in general, the text and why); the chair in office today **approves or declines** it with a fresh step-up.
- An approval writes a CORRECTION approval in the committee's chain and asks for **record version n+1**: the same minutes' pages as version 1, plus a Corrections annex. Earlier versions stay locked and readable (`?version=`); `minutes.record.superseded` is audited. A reader recused from the corrected item sees that a correction exists, not its text.

## Certified extracts, and how operations check them

- **Issuing:** a secretary of the committee in office, not recused from the item, once the decision has reached its memo (or the meeting is held, for an item without a memo), with a step-up. Refusals in order: 404, 403 `NOT_SECRETARY`, 403 `RECUSED`, 409 `VOID`, 409 `NOT_RELEASED`. The `minutes-extract` job makes a one-page PDF/A: the committee, meeting, item, number, outcome, text, conditions, validity, whether the minutes are approved ("have not yet been approved" before), and the certification with the extract's id and fingerprint. Never the vote, names or discussion.
- **Checking an extract someone shows you:**
  1. Open the resolution in memo (the number is on the extract) and its **Extracts** list.
  2. Compare the extract's id and fingerprint with the list; the list also shows its SHA-256, who issued it and when.
  3. Hash the file (`sha256sum extract.pdf`) and compare with the SHA-256 shown. Equal: it is the file memo issued.
  4. If the resolution was corrected since, the list says so; a resolution corrected while its extract was being made fails `CHANGED` (409 `EXTRACT_FAILED`): issue it again.
- Every issue and open is audited (`minutes.extract.requested`, `.issued`, `.opened`); the weekly fixity run re-hashes them.

## Monitoring

| Signal                                                                   | Meaning                                                              | Action                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `minutes.decision.release_failed`, `DECISIONS_RELEASE_FAILED`            | A memo did not move on after its tries                               | See Release above                                                                                             |
| `minutes.draft.failed` (`INVALID_TEMPLATE`, `MISSING_VALUE`, `CONTROLS`) | The template does not fill, or a duplicated block cannot be refilled | Fix the committee type's template, or make the whole draft again                                              |
| `minutes.copy.failed` with `LEAK`                                        | A redacted item's text is outside its block                          | Move it into its block (see Templates)                                                                        |
| `minutes.copies.refused`                                                 | More than 50 distinct redaction sets                                 | Fewer restrictions, or fewer readers asked for comments                                                       |
| `minutes.record.failed`                                                  | The record or an approved copy could not be made after its tries     | Bring ONLYOFFICE or storage back, then escalate: nothing asks for the record again (see The record and fixity) |
| `minutes.record.created` with `compliant: false`                         | veraPDF rejected the record; it was kept and flagged                 | Look at the failed rules (auditor's view)                                                                     |
| Any `*.mismatch`                                                         | A stored record, copy or extract changed                             | Incident (see fixity)                                                                                         |
| The register Check not "complete"                                        | A gap or duplicate in resolution numbers                             | Incident: numbers come from a locked counter; check the audit for `minutes.resolution.numbered` and `.voided` |

## Known limits

- The minutes' text is English only (the Arabic built-in template is shipped, not chosen yet).
- The sweep has no safety net that re-schedules a lost release job; the job's own retries and the failure notice stand in for it.
- A decision recorded in the seconds between the meeting's end and its job can take its number before the batch (the batch stays in agenda order).
- Records and extracts carry no cryptographic seal yet (PAdES is the upgrade path, as for memo records).
- The demo's seeded approvals and extract record no authentication factors: the seed acts outside a session.
- A record or approved copy that failed after its last try is never asked for again (no retry action or sweep); the web says "It is tried again", which is true only while its tries last.
- The decision register lists every decision under its own rule ([decision-register-and-analytics.md](decision-register-and-analytics.md)); its export carries the same number check.
