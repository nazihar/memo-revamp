# E03 Meetings and the agenda builder — log

## 2026-11-18
- **Plan written under the delegated gate** (Phase 4 Q1):
  - research: R1 versions; R2 from the help centres and reviews of eight portals (Convene, iBabs and Boardvantage publish almost nothing), the CGI and Board Intelligence 2026 board-reporting guidance, BoardSource and Board Intelligence on consent agendas, the bank terms of reference of Bank of Cyprus, Emirates NBD and HSBC, Basel 2015 and ORIC on conflicts, and WCAG 2.2 on dragging;
  - ADR-0060 (meetings and the agenda model) and ADR-0061 (meeting papers and the one access rule);
  - design, threat model, tasks T1–T9, test plan, and screen flows N–R for the owner with E02's flow M.
- **Choices that came from the research:**
  - A working draft with immutable published revisions and "What changed since you last looked" (Boardable, BoardPro, OnBoard).
  - Three levels, with items numbered continuously and renumbered only at publication.
  - The chair agrees the first publication (CGI, Emirates NBD §4.1.3), and the secretary may record it on the chair's behalf.
  - The paper deadline is five working days by default (CGI 2026, Bank of Cyprus §5.2), with E02's late modes enforced at placement.
  - Any member can lift a consent item without a reason or approval (BoardSource, Board Intelligence). No portal documents this inside the software.
  - "Only these people" restrictions on items and papers that survive replacement (against Diligent's re-permissioning complaint). Titles stay visible unless hidden (Admincontrol, Diligent Community).
  - Every move is a command before it is a drag (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.7, Diligent's "Move to").
  - dnd-kit 6.3.1 / sortable 10.0.0 pinned rather than the pre-1.0 `@dnd-kit/react`.
- **Changes from the overview, recorded in ADRs:**
  - **Members read the papers, not the memo** (ADR-0061). The overview assumed members would become memo readers through ADR-0050's reader table. That would give them the whole memo file (note sheet, every version, annexes left out of the pack) and put recusal into the memo rule. To report to the owner with the flows.
  - **Meetings live in the `meeting` module** (`AGENTS.md §4`), not in `forum`. The forum module keeps the agenda template on the type version (`forum/V5`) and publishes its events, `ForumPolicies` and `SubmissionPlacements`, so the dependency runs meeting → forum only.
  - The overview's meeting status "draft → scheduled → in progress → held → minutes approved → closed" is kept. E03 builds it up to held and cancelled.
- **Found while reading:**
  - The forum events (`ForumChanged`, `ForumTypePublished`, `ForumSubmissionChanged`) are in `forum.internal`, so no other module can listen to them yet. T1 moves them.
  - The web has no dnd-kit yet: the workflow builder uses React Flow.
  - `FilePurpose.mayRead` sees the file, not the version, so pinned paper versions are served through a new `FileServing` entry point behind the meeting rule.

  - `ForumChanged` was only published for the forum row; it is now also published for every membership change, which the audience needs. ## 2026-11-18 — T2 the agenda draft (backend)
- **Built:**
  - `meeting/V2` (`meeting_agenda`, `meeting_section`, `meeting_item`, `meeting_item_attendee`, `meeting_paper`; RLS forced, `tenant_id` first, composite foreign keys, no DELETE or TRUNCATE for the app role; the unique live placement `(tenant_id, submission_id) WHERE removed_at IS NULL`; one receiving section per agenda). jOOQ, the module metadata (meeting now also depends on memo, files and workflow), the contract and the web client regenerated.
  - The agenda policy on the committee type (flow R): `AgendaPolicy` (the chair's agreement and the standard agenda) in `com.memo.forum`, part of every type version (stored in `forum_type_version.agenda`, null read as the defaults), checked under `agenda.*` (30 sections, 150 items, titles, minutes 0–600, one receiving section, each standing kind once, 60 KB), in the presets (boards and board committees: Opening with the four standing items, For decision, Consent items, For noting, Any other business; management committees: the same without the consent section), in the version response and request, in the audit summary and in `ForumTypePolicy`. The web keeps it as loaded on save until the Agenda tab (T7).
  - Starting an agenda (`POST /meetings/{id}/agenda`): from the pinned type version's template, or a copy of an earlier meeting of a committee the actor is an officer of (sections, titles, kinds, purposes, times, presenters, attendees and restrictions narrowed to the new audience and listed; never submissions, papers, lateness, agreement or requests).
  - The builder: sections (add, rename, consent, members only, "new items land here", remove when empty), items, one level of sub-items and breaks (`ONE_LEVEL`), moves as commands (`POST …/agenda/moves`: an item to a position, a section or under an item; a section to a position), numbers and times computed on the draft (breaks and items waiting for the chair skipped), section totals and the overrun; presenters (users or names), per-item attendees (who join the audience as ATTENDEE), restrictions to people in the audience who are cleared, hidden titles and classifications never below the meeting's (nor the memo's, for a submission item) nor above the actor's clearance; the caps (422 `TOO_MANY`); If-Match on the agenda's version under its row lock (412), Idempotency-Key on creates, a per-user rate limit on builder writes (`AGENDA_WRITE_PER_USER`, 120 a minute).
  - The tray (`GET /forums/{fid}/submissions/ready?on=`, in the forum module: accepted, unplaced, due by the meeting first) and placing (`POST …/agenda/items` with `kind: SUBMISSION`): a queued submission is accepted in the same step; the late mode against the paper deadline (`WorkingDays.before`, in the meeting's zone): ALLOW → LATE, BLOCK → 409 `PAPER_DEADLINE_PASSED` before anything changes, CHAIR_APPROVAL → AWAITING_CHAIR with `meeting.late.requested` and `MEETING_LATE_PAPER` to the chair (the vice-chair when there is none; English and Arabic, no title); tabled while the meeting is in progress. The item takes the memo's subject, the cover sheet's purpose, resolution and presenter, and the memo's final record (or document) and attachments as papers.
  - `ForumSubmissionChanged` keeps drafts in step: returned, declined, referred, deferred or withdrawn → the item leaves the draft of a meeting not yet started (`SUBMISSION_CHANGED`) and the forum clears the placement.
  - Papers: memo papers (`MemoPapers`, new published API of the memo module, no access check) left out of and put back into the pack; uploads of the new `meeting.paper` purpose (record storage, PDF and text renditions, reads audited; secretaries upload while the meeting is not held; officers cleared for the meeting and the paper read them until T4); 20 per item; a new version of an upload is `meeting.agenda.paper_replaced` (refused once the meeting is held or cancelled); uploads follow the late rule, memo papers ride with their item.
  - `GET /meetings/{id}/agenda/draft` for officers (the chair reads, does not change): the draft with numbers, times, totals, overrun, deadline, tray count, papers with their files and the memo files out of the pack; before it is started, the template it would start from.
  - Tests: `AgendaRulesTest` (unit), `AgendaBuilderIT`, `AgendaPlacementIT`, `AgendaConcurrencyIT`, agenda policy in `ForumTypesIT`, agenda tables in `MeetingCrossTenantIT`; matrix `p4-e03-agenda.yaml`.
- **Decided here: what cancelling a meeting does to its agenda** (T1 left it open; design §1.1): the agenda freezes (`FROZEN` for every change), and its submission items are removed with the reason `MEETING_CANCELLED` and their placements cleared, so the submissions go back to the tray still accepted and can be placed on another meeting (`ONE_PLACEMENT` counts live items only). The other items stay as they were. Audited as `meeting.agenda.item_removed` per item, `forum.submission.unplaced` per submission and `meeting.agenda.released` once. A rescheduled meeting's placements follow its new date (`SubmissionPlacements.redate`, recorded by the meeting's own `meeting.rescheduled`).
- **Deviations from the design** (recorded in `design.md` as "as built"):
  - The tables are `meeting_agenda`, `meeting_section`, `meeting_item`, `meeting_item_attendee` and `meeting_paper`, not `agenda*`: every table is prefixed with its module (ADR-0006, `RlsMetaTest`). T3's revision tables follow (`meeting_revision*`).
  - FROZEN is not a stored agenda state: it follows the meeting's status (`changes`: ANY, TABLED_ONLY, NONE in the draft).
  - Every builder change answers the whole draft with its new version as the ETag, not only the new order.
  - The tray endpoint is the forum module's (`/forums/{fid}/submissions/ready`), so forum tables stay in their module; the draft counts it through `SubmissionPlacements.ready`.
  - Removing an item never removes its sub-items: they take its place. A section is removed only when empty (`SECTION_NOT_EMPTY`).
  - A copy leaves out an item whose restriction would name nobody on the new meeting (a restriction only narrows); it is listed with `skipped`.
  - Late uploads under CHAIR_APPROVAL wait as AWAITING_CHAIR on the paper; memo papers never are late on their own (they ride with their item).
  - Item attendees always include the user presenters; a standing kind is on an agenda at most once.
  - Members cannot read `meeting.paper` files yet: the purpose's `mayRead` is officers-only until T4 makes it the paper rule.
  - A builder change before the agenda is started answers 409 `NO_AGENDA`; raising an item's level raises its papers that were below it (a paper is never below its item).
  - The tray endpoint has its own controller (`SubmissionTrayController`), keeping `SubmissionsController` within detekt's function limit.
  - The draft is kept in step with the forum by a synchronous `ForumSubmissionChanged` listener, which locks the agenda after the forum has locked the submission, while a placement locks the agenda first. A return racing a placement of the same submission can therefore deadlock; PostgreSQL aborts one of them and the secretary retries. Accepted for now (the window is a few milliseconds); to revisit if it is ever seen.
