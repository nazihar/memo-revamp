Credit line renewal for Meghna Agro Ltd.

Credit proposal · Tanvir Ahmed · 14 November 2026

1. Summary

We propose to renew the revolving credit line of Meghna Agro Ltd. at BDT 250,000,000 for twelve months, on the same pricing. The client has repaid on time for 36 months.

2. Security

A first charge over the Narsingdi warehouse and hypothecation of stock. The October 2026 valuation gives security cover of 1.4×.

3. Financial position

Revenue grew 11% in FY2025 to BDT 1.9 billion; the current ratio stands at 1.35 and interest cover at 3.2×.

4. Recommendation

Renew at the proposed limit, subject to a review of security cover at six months.
