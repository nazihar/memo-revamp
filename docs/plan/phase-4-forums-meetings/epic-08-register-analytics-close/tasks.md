## Acceptance criteria (selection; the full list is in `test-plan.md`)
- **T1:**
  - Given BRC 3/2026 with item 5 decided "Approved with conditions" as BRC/2026/014, when the decision is recorded, then `search_decision` has its row in the same transaction, and `memo_app` cannot select from the table.
  - When the decision is corrected, the row shows the new text; when it is voided, the row shows VOID.
  - Given decisions recorded before T1's deployment, when the app starts, the backfill fills them and `search_decision_unindexed()` is empty.
  - The property test finds no viewer for whom the function's FULL, LINE or NONE differs from E06's rule.
- **T2:**
  - Given `director@` recused from item 5, when they search "Karnaphuli", they get no row; when they search "BRC/2026/014", they get the restricted line "BRC 3/2026, item 5 — Resolution BRC/2026/014 — Restricted item: you declared an interest"; the outcome facet does not count it.
  - When `director@` declares an interest in item 7 and searches at once, item 7 is a line.
  - `auditor@` (`forum.read`) sees item 6 (restricted to two members) as a line and every unrestricted item in full; a former member gets 403.
  - A search for 201 characters answers 422 `TEXT_TOO_LONG`; the 31st search in a minute answers 429.
- **T3:**
  - Given `auditor@` filtering BRC for 2026, when they export XLSX, the file has the Decisions, About this export and Number check sheets; the check shows 1..14 with none missing; a title starting with `=` starts with `'`; the audit entry holds the filters, 14 rows and the hash.
  - A member exporting gets 403; 20,001 rows answer 422 `TOO_MANY_ROWS`.
- **T4:**
  - Given BRC's 12 held meetings in the last year and `director2@` present at 10, absent with apologies at 1 (alternate attended) and absent at 1, then their row says "10 of 12 (83 %)", "1 represented by alternate", "1 absent".
  - `director@` (a member) sees their own row and the committee's total only.
  - Given 3 overdue BRC actions, one from item 5, `director@` sees 2 overdue; `secretary@` sees 3.
  - The median time to decision ignores the deferred decision on item 8 and counts its later approval from the first submission.
- **T5:** register search with text p95 < 300 ms for every profile at 108,000 decisions; analytics summary p95 < 800 ms; no sequential scan of `search_decision` in any plan.
- **T8:** `committee-cycle.spec` passes in CI and against dev, and `CommitteeCycleIT` verifies the audit chain after the cycle.
