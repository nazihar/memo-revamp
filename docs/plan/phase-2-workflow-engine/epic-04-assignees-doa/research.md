## R6 — Delegation of Authority (DoA) matrices

### How banks and large organisations structure and maintain them

| Aspect | What the sources show | Source type |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | A grid of **role/position × transaction type × amount band**, anchored in a board-approved policy. There are no universal thresholds: each institution sets its own | Practitioner guides (Tallyfy, Stampli) |
| Bands and escalation | Sample bank payment hierarchy: one approver in the lowest band, a supervisor added in the next, several senior approvers including compliance above USD 1,000,000. The top band ends at a committee or the board | GTB knowledge base sample |
| Combined signatures | Signatory classes with combination rules for the higher bands: "any two (2) A signatories", or one B with one A. Above a set value, dual approval is normal for outgoing payments | Security Bank DigiBanker help; NeuGroup peer note |
| Scope | Mandates cover who can approve disbursements, transfer limits, dual-signatory thresholds and **who can change the signatories** | Practitioner guides |
| Maintenance | Version control: date every version, keep superseded versions retrievable, record who approved each change. Review whenever signers, relationships or thresholds change, plus a regular review of signatory lists | Tallyfy; Charity Law Group policy |
| Currency | Coupa stores each approver limit as *amount + currency code*. No source describes cross-currency band rules; **not found** | Coupa approval chain import |
| Effective dating | Generic advice only ("date every version"). Effective-from dates in the products: **not found** in the sources read | — |

**Bangladesh Bank (primary text found).** BRPD Circular No. 11 of 27 October 2013 (reproduced in BB's *Prudential Regulations for Banks: Selected Issues*, January 2014) says:
- **Credit:** "The board shall specifically distribute the power of sanction of loan/investment and such distribution should desirably be made among the CEO and his subordinate executives as much as possible. No director, however, shall interfere, direct or indirect, into the process of loan approval."
- **Expenditure:** the board "shall frame the policies and procedures for bank's purchase and procurement activities and shall accordingly approve the distribution of power for making such expenditures. The maximum possible delegation of such power of expenditures shall rest on the CEO and his subordinates." Infrastructure, land, buildings and vehicles still need the board's approval.
- **Executive committee:** at most 7 board members; acts "within power delegated by the board"; "All decisions taken in the executive committee should be ratified in the next board meeting."
- The BB Credit Risk Management guidelines (revised by BRPD Circular 04 of 2016) have sections on approval authority and segregation of duties. Secondary copies say loans outside the lending guidelines go to the Head of Credit, the MD/CEO or the board. The official text was **not read** (bb.org.bd rejected automated fetches).
- A specific BB format for a DoA schedule (columns, bands): **not found**.

### "Who approves this?" in products

| Product | How authority is expressed | Simulator / preview |
|---|---|---|
| SAP MM release strategy | Characteristics from structure CEKKO (document type, total value, plant and so on) in a class (type 032). These select a strategy, which is an ordered set of release codes (approver levels) with release indicators. If the value rises past a tolerance, the document has to be released again | Release simulation in customizing. SAP KBA 2518872 notes that the simulation can disagree with the strategy after a transport |
| SAP Flexible Workflow | Step preconditions plus agent rules. Teams and Responsibilities: teams own business processes and members hold **functions**; responsibility rules become agent rules | No simulator found in the sources read |
| Oracle AME (EBS) / Fusion | Rule conditions on attribute ranges (e.g. amount 0–5000, inclusive). Action types climb the supervisor or **position hierarchy** to N levels, to a named position, or until an approver with a sufficient job level is found. Approver groups hold functional approvers | **AME Test Workbench**: load a real transaction by id and see the final approver list with the rules that applied. It cannot simulate forwarding, and a MOSC thread reports a live result differing from the test |
| SAP Ariba | Approval rules per approvable type produce an approval graph of users and groups, serial or parallel; watchers are notify-only | **Approval flow preview** before submit. It updates when lines or amounts change; the mobile app hides it for parallel nodes |
| Coupa | Approval chains per document type with min/max total ranges; up to 10 approver slots of *login:amount:currency*; self-approval limits per role | Approval chain shown on the requisition; insert-before/after approver actions |
| ServiceNow | Approval rules in Flow Designer over users, groups and scripts | No DoA simulator found |

**Recommendation:** model the DoA as **tenant data, separate from definitions**. Each row holds memo type, unit scope (unit or subtree), currency, amount band `[min, max)`, and a **requirement**: positions or levels with a count ("1 of GM", "any 2 of {DMD, AMD}", "1 A + 1 B"). The top band ends in a named committee or board step; nothing is unbounded.
- **Lifecycle:** draft → submitted → approved by a second administrator (maker ≠ checker) → **effective from** a date. The previous version is superseded and kept readable. A memo pins the DoA version in force when it is submitted, the same way it pins its definition version (E03).
- **Bands:** one currency per row, and no implicit FX conversion. A memo in a currency with no band fails resolution with a clear error.
- **Simulator** in the admin UI ("who approves a capex memo of BDT 7.5 crore from Branch X?"): returns the matched row, the version, and the resolved people per step. The same simulator runs as a read-only preview for the initiator before submit, as Ariba's does. As in AME, it cannot predict forwards or later delegations; the screen says so.
- The board and executive-committee levels follow BRPD 11/2013. Executive-committee approvals carry a "ratification pending" flag until the board meeting records it; that flag is a later enhancement unless the pilot asks for it.

## R12 — Separation of duties and conflict of interest in approvals

### Frameworks and what auditors test

| Source | What it requires |
|---|---|
| GAO Green Book (GAO-14-704G) §10.12–10.14 | Separate "authorizing transactions, processing and recording them, reviewing the transactions, and handling any related assets so that no one individual controls all key aspects". Where segregation is not practical (limited staff), "management designs alternative control activities". It names management override and collusion as limits |
| COSO 2013, Principle 10 point of focus | "Management segregates incompatible duties, and where such segregation is not practical management selects and develops alternative control activities" (secondary quotation; the COSO text itself was not read) |
| ISACA Journal (2016, 2022) | Incompatible duties are ones a single actor should not perform on the same asset; compensating controls are needed where SoD cannot be enforced. Full article text **not read** (excerpts only) |
| Audit testing practice | Review SoD violation reports and logs; check that changes to the rules are themselves separated (maker-checker on configuration); have an independent party periodically test that compensating controls work. These are secondary sources; an IIA or PCAOB primary test script was **not found** |
| Bangladesh Bank BRPD 11/2013 | No director may interfere in loan approval; board members stay out of HR selection committees |
| EBA loan origination guidelines (EBA/GL/2020/06, §4.4, paras 63–71) | Anyone involved in a credit decision with an economic or other interest in the borrower (direct or indirect, actual or potential) **abstains**; policies define the controls. Secondary reproduction; final paragraph wording not read |
| Credit-union analogues (US state law) | A committee member may not vote on a loan they guarantee or in which family have an interest; minutes list the decisions |

### How products express SoD

| Product | Rule types | Exceptions |
|---|---|---|
| SAP GRC Access Control | Rule set → **access risks** of three kinds: SoD (two or more conflicting functions), critical action, critical permission. Each risk is rated Low/Medium/High/Critical | **Mitigating controls** from a catalogue are assigned to a user or role that holds a risk |
| SAP Flexible Workflow | Step property "Exclude restricted agents" (e.g. exclude the requester or the PO creator). KBA 3625737: if the only agent is excluded, the workflow goes into error. Classic workflow sends recipients to possible ∩ responsible − excluded agents | An orphan item goes to the workflow administrator |
| Oracle Fusion / PeopleSoft approvals | "Prohibit User Self-Approval" (replaced "skip creator" in 20A). PeopleSoft self-approval criteria: when met, the requester's approval is assumed and the step's minimum count drops by one. Otherwise routing moves to the next step, or to the **administrator** if none is left | Criteria-based self-approval |
| Coupa | Self-approval limits per role. Spend Guard checks self-approved requisitions against the limits of both the requester and the manager, which catches requisitions split to stay under a limit | — |
| Declared interests | No product source found that captures an approver's declared interest per transaction; **not found** | — |

**Recommendation:** a fixed catalogue of **SoD rules**, each with an id, a severity and a tenant on/off switch where allowed. Every evaluation is recorded with the memo.
- *SOD-1* The initiator or preparer never approves (exists in E01; not switchable).
- *SOD-2* The same person does not approve twice in one chain (default on).
- *SOD-3* An acting-for delegate cannot approve a memo they initiated, or one their principal is excluded from.
- *SOD-4* **Declared interest:** at the task, the assignee declares an interest with a reason and is recused. The task re-resolves to the next eligible holder, and the recusal is visible in the history. A tenant-maintained related-party register is later.
- *SOD-5* Nobody approves changes to the DoA, positions or SoD rules they made themselves (maker-checker on configuration).

When a rule leaves a step with nobody eligible, the step goes to the administrator queue (R5); it is **never skipped or self-approved**. An override is an exception: it needs a justification, step-up authentication and a second administrator's approval, and it appears in an **SoD exceptions report**. That report is what an auditor samples: approved memos with initiator ≠ approvers, each approver authorised under the DoA version in force, every exception justified and approved.

## R5 (part) — Staff changes while a memo is in flight

| Situation | SAP | Oracle | ServiceNow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planned absence | My Inbox substitution rules, **planned** (dated) and **unplanned** (pre-defined, inactive until the substitute activates it). Substitutes come from HR relationship A210 or table HRUS_D2; the buffer usually refreshes at midnight | **Vacation rules** (start/end) with two modes. **Delegate:** the task stays assigned to the original user. **Reassign/Transfer:** ownership moves completely. A request for more information can only be transferred. A workflow may forbid reassignment, and then the rule is ignored | **Delegates** with start/end dates and separate approvals / assignments / notifications switches. Zurich adds granular delegation with rule conditions |
| Position-based assignment | Agents come from org objects or responsibility rules at the moment the item is created | AME position hierarchy notifies **every holder** of the position; the first to respond wins. Community and support threads report that vacation rules do not apply reliably to position-hierarchy approvals | Group or role based |
| Assignee leaves | No automatic re-resolution described for items already created (**not found**) | Not found in documentation | No platform behaviour: community scripts clear `assigned_to` or move the item back to the group. By default approvals are still generated for inactive approvers (`com.glide.hub.flow.approval.allow_inactive_entity`) |
| Shared queue | Work item to several recipients: **Claim** reserves it, **Release** returns it to all original recipients. KBA 3145611: a release under substitution may not return the item to the originals | BPM Worklist: a group or multi-user task must be **claimed** first (unless auto-claim is set) and can be **released** for others. Fusion Financials releases a claimed task back to the group automatically after a time limit | Assignment group plus `assigned_to` |
| No assignee found | Orphan work item (all agents excluded or none found) goes to the workflow administrator (SWI2_ADM1). Flexible workflow raises an error when the creator is the only agent | AME `ALLOW_EMPTY_APPROVAL_GROUPS` decides whether an empty group is allowed. PeopleSoft routes to the administrator when no step is left | Behaviour varies: the flow stalls, the step is marked **Skipped**, or the approval has no approvers (community reports). Docs advise due dates and rejection rules |

**Recommendation:**
- **Hold by position.** A task resolved from a position belongs to the position. When the incumbent changes, open tasks **re-resolve** to the new holder, with a history entry ("re-assigned: holder of GM-Credit changed"). A task forwarded to a named person falls back to that person's position when they leave.
- **Absence.** Planned absence uses the dated acting-for delegation from 1b (Oracle's *delegate* mode: the task stays the principal's and the delegate acts for them, subject to SOD-3). The holder can **forward** explicitly; that is Oracle's *reassign* mode.
- **Queues.** A task for several eligible people (e.g. any 2 of DMD/AMD, or a unit queue) uses **claim/release**, with an optional automatic release after N hours in E05.
- **Nobody found.** Never skip or auto-approve. The step goes to the tenant's **workflow administrator queue** with an alert, and the memo shows "awaiting assignment". Resolution failures appear on the E05/E06 dashboards.
