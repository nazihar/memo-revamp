# E04 — UX review

The per-epic gate is delegated for Phase 2 (overview §10, Q1). E04's screens follow E01 and E03 patterns; findings from the next demo session are recorded here.

## Flows
1. **Resolved approver:** memo view → current step "Credit review — Rahim Uddin, holder of GM Credit". If the holder changes, the history says so.
2. **Shared task:** three candidates see the memo in their inboxes ("Any of 3"). One chooses **Claim**; the others see "Claimed by Rahim" and cannot act. **Release** puts it back.
3. **Declare an interest:** on an open task, **Declare an interest** → reason → the task leaves; the step goes to the next eligible person; the history shows the recusal without the reason (the reason is in the remark, readable under the memo's rules).
4. **DoA preview (initiator):** before submit, "Who approves this?" lists the groups (2 of DMD, AMD → names).
5. **DoA maintenance (admin A, admin B):** Admin → DoA → New version → rules table (scope, condition, groups) → Simulate → Submit; B reviews the changes against the published version → Approve (code) with an effective date.
6. **Route with positions and groups:** the route editor adds a person, a position, or a group (all / any); entries outside the allowed approvers are not offered.
7. **Unresolved:** the reassigners get "A memo is waiting for reassignment"; Open tasks shows "Nobody found for GM Credit (vacant)"; Reassign as E01.
