# E02 Submissions and the escalation link — log

## 2026-11-18
- Plan written under the delegated gate: research (eScribe, Diligent Community, Boardable, CivicPlus, Legistar; bank committee charters; EBA/GL/2020/06), ADR-0058 (the waiting forum step), ADR-0059 (submissions and triage), design, threat model, tasks T1–T5, test plan, flow M for the owner with E03.
- One change from the overview: a decision follows a fixed mapping (approve or noted continues, reject rejects, return or decline returns). The overview also offered a CEL attribute to branch on the outcome; that is deferred, because no source needed it.
- T1 (the waiting forum step) merged in #159; T2 (submissions and triage) merged in #160. CI on #160 found that `DefinitionRuntimeIT` escalated to a committee its tenant never had; the test now creates it, since an escalation to an unknown committee is refused (`FORUM_INACTIVE`).
- T3 (readers and the memo side), with these differences from the design:
  - **No system note kind.** A committee's return or decline is already a workflow action with the secretary as actor and the reason as its remark, and the note sheet already lists such actions. The note sheet entry now also carries `committee` (the name) and `committeeOutcome`, so it reads "Returned by the Management Credit Committee: reason". A second, system-written entry would have recorded the same thing twice.
  - **The submission policy got its own migration** (`forum/V4`), not a V3 column: V3 had merged without it. Null reads as the defaults (5 working days, chair approval for late papers, a reminder after 5 working days).
  - **Committee readers are kept in step, not granted once.** `MemoReaders.syncForum` makes the memo's `FORUM` rows exactly the chair and secretaries in office of every forum with an open submission of the memo. It runs after every submission change and after a chair or secretary joins or leaves. A role that starts on a future date takes effect at the next change. `FORUM` is the weakest participant kind, so it never replaces a stronger row, and retiring it leaves other rows alone.
  - **"With the X committee"** reaches the memo through the workflow, not the forum module (`WorkflowForums.waitingOn`, names from `EscalationTargets`). This keeps the direction forum → memo and workflow.
  - **Fixed a T1 defect:** a step's `resolution` was written but never read back, so a reloaded waiting step had forgotten its forum. The forum's decision action then had no `forum` detail. Saving any changed step also blanked the resolution E04 records (who an assignee resolved to, and why). `InstanceStore` now reads it back.
- T5 (close, backend part):
  - **Demo:** "Credit under authority" gets a new version, maker-checked like the rest. After the credit desk, a branch on the proposal's amount sends BDT 10 million or more to the Management Credit Committee (`escalate-to-forum`, `wait: true`) before the delegation of authority; smaller amounts go straight to the authority. The seeder seeds the committees first and adds this version last, after the credit proposal type that brings `amount`. `WorkflowSeeding.revise` now revises the latest published version, not only a lone version 1. `DemoWorkflowsIT` walks both amounts through the simulator.
  - **Validation fix:** when two subject types offer one attribute name with different types, a definition's declared type now counts if any of them offers it. Before, the last subject type registered won, which in tests made the memo's `amount` (a double) clash with the test subject's (an int).
  - **Performance** (`SubmissionPerformanceIT`): 50,000 submissions, 2,000 open in one queue. Queue (500 rows with names and readable memos): p95 44 ms against a budget of 150 ms. A memo's submissions: p95 10 ms against 50 ms. Both use an index.
  - Runbook `committees.md`; four ASVS rows (V8 ×2, V2, V16).
  - **Moved to T4:** the journey `committee-escalation.spec` drives the queue screens, which wait for the owner's review of flow M with E03. A seeded paper in the demo queue moves there too: seeding one needs memo field values, which the seeding API does not take yet.
  - **Committee threshold set at BDT 10 million, above the DoA band (BDT 5 million).** At 5 million the smoke journey `authority.spec` (an 8 million proposal through the director and the chief financial officer) stopped at the committee, which has no screens until T4. A committee above the top authority band is also the usual shape in banks: the committee sees the largest exposures, the authority bands cover the rest. The T4 journey uses a proposal of BDT 12 million.
- T4 (web: committee papers), after the owner approved flow M as recommended on 2026-11-18:
  - **Committee page → Submissions** (`/committees/$forumId?tab=submissions`), for the chair, secretaries in office and `forums.manage`; the tab shows the open count. Filters Waiting, Accepted, Deferred (counts from the open list) and Closed (fetched only when chosen). Columns: purpose, memo number and subject linked to the memo or "Restricted", sender, age, flags (waiting too long, for information only, sent again), status, and a Triage menu for secretaries and administrators; the chair gets "Open the paper".
  - **Triage dialogs** (accept, defer, return, decline, refer, cover sheet) say what each does to the memo, need a reason where the server does (checked in the browser and mapped from `REASON_REQUIRED`), send `If-Match`, and reload the lists after a refusal (`WRONG_STATE`, 412, `SAME_FORUM`). Accept and the cover sheet show the type's deadline and late-paper rule in words. The Accept dialog has no meeting picker; E03's "Accept and place on an agenda" (O6) adds it.
  - **A paper's page** `/committees/$forumId/submissions/$submissionId`, where the `FORUM_SUBMISSION_*` notifications lead. It needs only the paper, since the author cannot see the committee itself. Officers who triage get the actions there as well.
  - **The memo:** the committee panel (each paper newest first, with its timeline and what comes next), "With the Management Credit Committee" where the holder would be named, the same in the Sent box, and note-sheet lines "Returned by the Management Credit Committee". A new catalogue part `papers` (`/memos`, `/committees`) holds the texts both pages share.
  - **Builder:** the escalate step's "Wait for the committee's decision" switch, on for new steps; a step saved before E02 reads as off. The steps view and the changes view show it.
  - **Committee types:** "Papers for meetings" on the Terms tab (papers due, late papers, the triage reminder), in the changes view and in the committee's Policy tab.
  - **Backend, three small fixes found by the screens:**
    - the forum's refer body collided with the memo's `ReferRequest` in the contract (springdoc keys schemas by simple name), so the generated client described `{to, remark}`; it is now `ReferSubmissionRequest`;
    - `remindedAt` on a submission, so the queue can flag "waiting too long" as design §3 asks;
    - `submissionPolicy` on the committee's policy, so the dialogs can state the deadline.
  - **Not in T4:** the "late" flag on a queue row. Lateness is measured against a meeting's paper deadline, which E03 defines (design E03 §1.4); until then the dialogs state the rule. A seeded paper in the demo queue is still carried (the seeding API does not take memo field values).
  - Journey `committee-escalation.spec` (BDT 12 million; the secretary accepts by keyboard; the initiator sees "Accepted for the meeting of …"). It passed locally against a full stack started from this branch (Postgres, SeaweedFS, Mailpit, ClamAV, Tika; no ONLYOFFICE). New e2e role `secretary`.
