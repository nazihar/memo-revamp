## Acceptance criteria (selection; the full list is in `test-plan.md`)
- **T1 (spike):** given the default template filled for the seeded BRC meeting with 9 items, when the file is opened in ONLYOFFICE 10.0.0.1, item 5's text edited, a deletion of item 6's control attempted and undone, and the file saved through the callback, then the saved file has 9 controls with their tags and locks, item 5's edit inside its control, and the copy with item 5 replaced has no word of item 5's discussion in its PDF text layer.
- **T2 (policy):** given a type draft with `approval` BY_CIRCULATION, `circulationWorkingDays` 0, when it is submitted, 422 `RANGE` at `minutes/circulationWorkingDays`; a template with `{{item.discusion}}` is refused with `UNKNOWN_TAG` and the suggestion `item.notes`.
- **T3 (decision):**
  - Given the BRC meeting IN_PROGRESS with item 5 in progress and 5 of 7 voting members present, when `secretary@` records "Approved with conditions" with one condition, a vote 4–1–0 and `director2@` dissenting with a reason, then the decision has a snapshot (quorate), took no part = the recused `director@`, and is audited.
  - Naming `director@` (recused) as abstaining answers 422 `RECUSED_NAMED`; a vote of 6–1–0 answers 422 `TOO_MANY_VOTES`.
  - A recused secretary recording item 5 gets 403 `RECUSED`.
  - Deciding the consent section writes one decision per remaining item, not for the lifted item 8.
- **T4 (numbers):**
  - Given decisions recorded on items 7, 5 and 9 (in that order), all resolutions, when the meeting is marked as held, they are numbered BRC/2026/001 for item 5, 002 for 7, 003 for 9.
  - Given 20 committees ending meetings at once and 50 concurrent decisions recorded after HELD, each committee's numbers are 1..n without gaps or duplicates, and a release forced to fail and roll back leaves no gap.
  - Correcting item 7 (numbered, not released, ON_MINUTES_APPROVAL) to NOTED voids BRC/2026/002 with the reason; the next number is 004.
  - With AT_MEETING_END, item 5's credit memo resumes at HELD, its note sheet shows "Management Credit Committee — Approved with conditions — Resolution MCC/2026/001", and its final record's approval history carries it; a later outcome correction answers 409 `RELEASED`.
  - DEFERRED puts the submission back to the tray with the target; REFERRED creates a linked submission to the other committee; the memo keeps waiting in both.
- **T5 (draft):** given the held meeting, when the secretary generates the draft, the .docx has one control per item, the present and apologies tables, item 5's declaration sentence and "took no part", and `minutes.status` "DRAFT"; deleting item 6's control in a saved version makes send-for-review answer 409 `STRUCTURE` naming item 6, and "Restore item 6" fixes it.
- **T6 (approval):**
  - Given circulated BRC minutes (AT_NEXT_MEETING), `director@` (recused from item 5) reads a copy whose item 5 shows the heading, their declaration and the decision, and no notes or discussion (canaries absent from the text layer); `director2@` reads the full copy.
  - When the next meeting decides "Approved" on its previous-minutes item, the minutes wait for the chair; when the chair confirms with a fresh step-up, the meeting becomes MINUTES_APPROVED, attendance and declarations are frozen, and the approval row's hash chains to the committee's previous one.
  - Given MCC minutes BY_CIRCULATION with a 5-working-day deadline, when the deadline passes, the sweep moves them to the chair once.
  - Confirming without a step-up answers the step-up problem; confirming by the vice-chair while the chair is able answers 403.
- **T7 (record):** the record validates as PDF/A-2u in veraPDF, is stored object-locked with its SHA-256 audited, and the fixity job reports it verified; `director@`'s approved copy is a separate locked record naming item 5 as redacted.
- **T8 (corrections, extracts):** an approved correction produces record version 2 with the original pages unchanged and a Corrections annex; version 1 stays readable; an extract of MCC/2026/001 says "The minutes of this meeting have not yet been approved" before approval and is refused for a VOID resolution.
