## 2026-11-28 — Officers' memo access follows terms and position holders (fix of the E08 T10 finding)
- **The defect:** the `FORUM` readers of a queued memo were brought in line only after a member edit to a chair or secretary row or a submission change (T3 above: "a role that starts on a future date takes effect at the next change"). So a term that lapsed on its end date left the former officer reading the queue's memos, an appointment that started on its date gave nothing, and a position seat's new holder read nothing while the previous holder kept reading, until the next edit. Shown first by a test on the unfixed code: after a day passed (term ended yesterday, the next started today) and the forum module's existing hourly work ran, the former secretary still read the memo (200, expected 404); after a position's assignment moved to a new holder, the new holder got 404.
- **The fix** (ADR-0059 amendment):
  - `OfficerReaders.daily`, run by the per-tenant task `forum-officer-readers` (the sweep `forum-officer-readers-sweep` fans out every 15 minutes): once per tenant day, at the first run after midnight in the tenant's zone, it resyncs the committees whose chair and secretaries differ between two consecutive days since the last run. The day is kept in `forum_reader_sync` (`forum/V9`, RLS enabled and forced, one row per tenant locked for the run). The first run, or one after more than 31 days, resyncs every committee, which also heals tenants where the gap already left wrong rows.
  - `OfficerReaders.onOrgChanged`: an assignment change resyncs, in the changing transaction, the committees with a chair or secretary seat of that position, and publishes `ForumChanged` for every committee with a seat of it, so open meetings recompute their audience.
  - `SubmissionReaders` takes the date explicitly (`sync`, `resync`, `officersOn`) and reads each forum's officers once per resync. Resyncing still diffs and audits through `MemoReaders.syncForum`: only changed rows are written, each change audited `memo.reader.forum_added` or `memo.reader.forum_retired`.
- **Other derived access checked:**
  - Meeting officers (chair, vice-chair when there is no chair, secretaries), paper officers, minutes and decision access, the decision register's officer scope, action viewers and the action module's memo reads: computed at read time from `ForumDirectory` and `MemoReaders.readable`. Not affected.
  - Meeting audiences (`meeting_audience`): materialised, computed for the meeting's date with `until` checked at read time, so terms by date were already followed. A position seat's holder change was not (no `ForumChanged`); now it is, for open meetings. Held meetings keep their audience; a position seat's `until` is the seat's last day. Recorded as a residual risk in the E03 threat model.
  - Action items' `holder_user_id`: follows the workflow's task for the owner's step, which the engine re-resolves on an assignment change (`StaffChangeListener`) but not when an assignment starts or ends on a recorded date. That is the workflow engine's position-step behaviour for every subject (memos too), not forum access; left for the workflow module as a follow-up.
  - Vice-chairs are not queue readers: ADR-0059 names the chair and secretaries, and the code agrees. Unchanged.
- Tests: `OfficerAccessIT` (term ended yesterday and term starting today across midnight in Asia/Dhaka, on a pinned `MutableClock`, still the same UTC day; a position seat's new holder at once; a holder change recorded for tomorrow at tomorrow's run; a second run the same day does nothing; cross-tenant isolation of `forum_reader_sync`), and `MeetingsIT` (a position seat's audience follows its holder).
