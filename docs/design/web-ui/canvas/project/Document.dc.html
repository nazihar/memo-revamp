Credit line renewal for Meghna Agro Ltd.

Credit proposal · Prepared by Tanvir Ahmed, Relationship Manager · 14 November 2026

1. Summary

We propose to renew the revolving credit line of Meghna Agro Ltd. at BDT 250,000,000 for twelve months, on the same pricing. The client has used the line within its limit throughout the last term and repaid on time for 36 months.

2. Security

The line is secured by a first charge over the Narsingdi warehouse and hypothecation of stock. The panel valuer’s report of October 2026 puts the market value of the warehouse at BDT 350,000,000, giving security cover of 1.4×.

3. Recommendation

Renew at the proposed limit, subject to a review of security cover at six months.
