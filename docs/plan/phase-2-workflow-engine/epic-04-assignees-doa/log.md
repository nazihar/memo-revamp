# E04 Assignees, routing rules and DoA — log

## 2026-11-12
- Plan written under the delegated gate (Phase 2 Q1): research (R1, R5 in part, R6, R12), ADR-0040, design, threat model, tasks T1–T10, test plan, UX review notes. Implementation starts with T1.
- Choices worth knowing: resolution happens when a step activates and is recorded on the step (who and why); several candidates share a step through claim and release; nobody found means a blocked task for the administrators, never a skipped step; the DoA is tenant data with its own maker-checker and effective dates, pinned per instance; substitutions live in `workflow` until Phase 1b's delegation model, which may take them over.
- Out of E04 scope, recorded for later: the SoD exceptions report and overrides with justification and a second administrator; a related-party register; automatic release of claimed tasks after a time (E05); "offered to both" substitution mode (only "goes to the substitute" now); executive-committee ratification flags (research R6).
- Org has no acting assignments yet (`org_assignment.kind` is `PRIMARY` only, 1b), so acting-for comes from substitutions in E04.
- **T1 (read facades):** `org.OrgDirectory` (a service in the module's public package over its stores: holders on a date, a person's position, positions by id, a unit's head and ancestors nearest first, the reporting line up to 10 levels) and `common.authz.RoleHolders` (grants tenant-wide or scoped to a unit or one above it, through the cached `OrgTree`). Both read only; account state is the caller's check. `OrgDirectoryIT` over a seeded organisation, with a future assignment, a vacant head and a role scoped to a sibling.
- **T2 (resolvers):** assignee kinds `position`, `supervisor`, `unitHead`, `role` (schema 2, additive; `unit` is `"initiator"` or an id) and a step `fallback` (a named person). The engine asks a `Resolver` port when it activates a person step (`Candidates`, then `Activations` shapes the step): one person holds it; several each get the task and the first decision completes the step (`workflow_step.needed` = 1; a parallel step keeps its mode's count); nobody (or fewer than a parallel step needs) holds the step with a task without assignee, blocked `UNRESOLVED`, which the reassigners hear about and assign with E01's reassignment. The initiator is never a candidate (SOD-1, recorded as an exclusion). Every resolved step records the resolution in `workflow_step.resolution` (kind, inputs, the people, exclusions, whether the fallback was used, and why nobody was found: `VACANT`, `NO_POSITION`, `NO_LINE`, `NO_UNIT`, `NO_HEAD`, `NOBODY`, `TOO_MANY_CANDIDATES` above 20). `OrgResolvers` answers from `OrgDirectory` and `RoleHolders`. Eligibility of resolved people is asked lazily, once per person per action. Task assignees, step holders and the task events' `assigneeId` became nullable (migration `workflow/V5`, with a CHECK that only `UNRESOLVED` tasks lack one). The validator refuses unknown positions, roles and units (`DefinitionReferences`), an initiator deciding, and a fallback that is not a person. Route entries record no resolution (the initiator chose them), so invariant 22 reads "every activated step from a definition assignee". The memo view does not show resolutions yet (T8). Tests: `ResolutionTest`, `ResolutionProperties` (invariants 19 and 22 over random resolver answers), `ResolversIT`, validator cases.
- **T3 (queues, claim and release):** assignee kind `queue` (by key; members are people or positions, resolved to today's holders) and queues as tenant data (`workflow_queue`, `workflow_queue_member`, migration `workflow/V6`, forced RLS) behind `/api/v1/admin/workflow/queues` (`workflow.queues.manage`, new, privileged; If-Match on changes; audited). A fallback may now be a queue. A step resolved to several candidates can be claimed (`CLAIM`, `POST /api/v1/tasks/{id}/claim`): the step's `claimed_by` is set, the claimer alone holds it (`current_assignees`), and the others' decisions are refused `CLAIMED_BY_OTHER` while their tasks stay open; `RELEASE` by the claimer, or by an administrator at `POST /api/v1/admin/workflow/tasks/{id}/release`. Parallel steps cannot be claimed (`NOT_SHARED`); a consultation on a claimed step still answers. The memo view offers Claim and Release and names the claimer (`TaskRef.shared`, `.claimedBy`); the buttons themselves come in T8. Claim and release endpoints sit in their own `MemoTaskClaimsController` (the tasks controller was at the function limit). Tests: `QueueClaimIT`, matrix `p2-e04-assignees.yaml`.
- **T4 (separation of duties):** resolution now leaves out, per rule and recorded in the step's `excluded`: the initiator (SOD-1); anyone who approved or recommended on another step of the round (SOD-2; tenant setting `workflow.sod.once.per.round`, default on; setting keys allow lower case and dots only, so the design's camel-case name changed); and people with a declared interest (SOD-4, `WorkflowSubject.interested`). SOD-2 counts decisions on other steps only, so a step that runs again after a send-back goes back to the person who decided it. A fallback never brings back an excluded person. The memo keeps interests in `memo_interest` (migration `memo/V4`; the reason stays there, never in the audit log): the initiator lists them on a draft or returned memo (`POST/DELETE /api/v1/memos/{id}/interests`, a reason required), and an interested person on the route is refused at route validation (`route[i]: interested`). A holder stands aside with `POST /api/v1/tasks/{id}/recuse` (reason required; the memo records their interest first): on a shared step the others carry on, otherwise the step resolves again without them (`RECUSE` action, migration `workflow/V7`), and a route entry, chosen by the initiator, waits for an administrator. SOD-3 (substitutes) comes with substitutions in T5. Audit: `memo.interest.declared`, `memo.interest.removed`, `workflow.task.recused`. Tests: `SeparationOfDutiesIT`, `MemoInterestsIT`; `ResolversIT`'s chain changed so one person no longer holds two of its steps.
- **T5 (substitution and staff changes):** substitutions as tenant data (`workflow_substitution`, migration `workflow/V8`, forced RLS; dates are the tenant's calendar dates, the end exclusive) behind `/api/v1/admin/workflow/substitutions` (`workflow.tasks.reassign` until Phase 1b brings self-service; the substitute must be an active user). At most one per principal at any date (`OVERLAP`, under an advisory lock). While one is in force, a task the engine would give the principal goes to the substitute with `on_behalf_of` set, so their decisions record `acting_for`; never when the substitute initiated the item or declared an interest in it (SOD-3, checked by the runner's `substituteOf`). Ending one today stops new tasks going to the substitute (one not yet started is deleted). `POST …/{id}/apply` (step-up) moves the principal's open tasks, up to 100, to the substitute as reassignments acting for the principal; ones the engine refuses stay and the count moved comes back. Staff changes (engine `Block`, `Reresolve`; `StaffChangeListener`): when an assignment of a position changes, an open step resolved from that position resolves again (vacant until someone holds it; `REASSIGN` with reason `HOLDER_CHANGED` and who held it); when a user is deactivated, their open tasks are replaced by tasks blocked `INACTIVE` for the administrators to reassign (`REASSIGN` with reason `INACTIVE`). Both are recorded as done by the administrator who changed the organisation; system-made changes (no signed-in user) are left to the administrators. Audit: `workflow.substitution.created`, `.ended`, `.applied`. Tests: `SubstitutionIT`, `StaffChangeIT`, matrix rows.
