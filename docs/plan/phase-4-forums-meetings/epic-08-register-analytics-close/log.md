## 2026-11-22 — T1: the register's table, indexer and functions
- **Migration `search/V4__decisions.sql`** (expand only): `search_decision`, one row per decision with its current entry. RLS enabled, not forced, owned by the schema owner, **no privilege for `memo_app`** (ADR-0054's pattern). Indexes, `tenant_id` first: GIN on `tsv` (`fastupdate`, 2 MB pending list); `(meeting_date, decided_at, decision_id)` across committees and the same per committee (newest first is the backward scan); `(forum_id, number_period, number_sequence, decision_id) WHERE resolution_number IS NOT NULL`; `ref_norm`, `ref_tail`, `memo_ref_norm`; `meeting_id` and `item_id` for the set-based updates. No foreign key to `minutes_decision` (another module's table).
- **What the row holds:** committee and type; meeting (label, local date); item (number, parent, title, the higher of the item's and the meeting's level, restricted-to, hidden title, members-only); outcome kind and label; resolution (id, the number in force else the last void one, IN_FORCE or VOID, period, sequence, `ref_norm`, `ref_tail`); state (RECORDED, NUMBERED, RELEASED, FINAL); decided and final times; entry number (`corrected` generated); text, conditions (`has_conditions` generated), valid until; `has_dissent`, vote mode, to be ratified and by which decision; submission, memo, memo type and number (`memo_ref_norm`); the chain's first submission, `deferred` (generated) and earlier deferrals on the chain; `body` (label, text, conditions; 16,000 characters) and `tsv` (title A, numbers B, body C). **Never:** the secretary's notes, the minutes' discussion, papers, natures of interests, dissenters' or voters' names, override reasons, recusals.
- **Definer functions** (search path pinned, EXECUTE for `memo_app` only, the tenant argument checked against `app.tenant_id`): writers `search_put_decision`, `search_put_decisions_final` (one write per meeting) and `search_put_decision_items` (the meeting's columns and each decided item's from a JSON array; two set-based updates); `search_decision_unindexed` (missing rows, and rows behind their decision: another current entry, or the minutes approved since); the reader `search_find_decisions` (one statement: the page, LINE rows without their content, then a META row with the total up to 10,000, `capped` and the facets; planned per call through `EXECUTE … USING` from `search_find_decisions_sql`, ADR-0054's amendment); `search_decision_stats` stubbed (FULL rows per committee) for T4. The rule is one inlined SQL function, `search_decision_level` (not callable by the app): design §1.3, every null parameter read as "none".
- **The indexer** (`search.internal.decision`, synchronous `@EventListener`s in the publisher's tenant transaction): `DecisionRecorded` (every entry, and the decisions it ratifies now or ratified before), `ResolutionChanged`, `DecisionReleased`, `MinutesApproved`, `AgendaPublished`, `MeetingChanged` (UPDATED, RESCHEDULED, NUMBERED). Rows are read from published APIs only (`Decisions`, `Resolutions`, `Meetings`, `MeetingItems`, `ForumDirectory`, `Clearances`, `MemoSearchSource`, `SubmissionChains`). E06's `DecisionRecorded` is published synchronously in the recording transaction (the plan's open question, checked), so no after-commit path is needed.
- **`MinutesApproved`:** #229 (E06 T6) is not merged yet. The event's class is added byte for byte as on its branch (`MinutesApproved(tenantId, meetingId, forumId)`), so the merge is clean; the listener makes the meeting's rows FINAL with the decisions' `final_at` (T6 sets it before publishing). Tested by publishing the event; the unindexed check also finds a row whose minutes were approved without it.
- **Published for E08:** `Recusals.itemsOf(userId)` (meeting: effective declarations, a pending ruling included, the member's when standing in as an alternate, sub-items); `SubmissionChains.chain` and `.chainStart` (forum); `Decisions.ofSubmissions`, `.ratifiers`, `.ids`, and `DecisionEntryRef.voteMode`, `.dissent`, `.ratifiesDecisionId` (minutes); `AgendaItemRef.classificationId` and `.membersOnly` are read as E07 T3 published them (meeting). The search module now depends on minutes, meeting and forum; nothing depends on search but `demo`.
- **`DecisionRegisterAccess`** computes the viewer's parameters per request: officer committees (secretary, chair, the vice-chair only when there is no chair: E06's `MeetingAccess.officer`), member committees (chair, vice-chair or member seat in office today), `forum.read`, `forums.manage`, clearance and `Recusals.itemsOf`. The published **`DecisionSearch`** (`scope()`, `find(DecisionQuery)` → `DecisionPage` of `RegisterRow`s with total, capped, facets and a keyset cursor) is what T2's API calls.
- **Jobs:** `search-decisions-backfill` per tenant: chunks of 500, each in its own transaction, up to 120 a run, resumable (the work list is what is still missing or behind), idempotent; with `rebuild` it walks every decision by id, each chunk scheduling the next (the runbook's rebuild); audited `search.decisions.backfilled` once per run. `search-decisions-check` (platform; at start-up, then daily; off in tests with the memo sweep's property) schedules it for every tenant; the backfill reports `memo.search.decisions.unindexed`.

## 2026-11-22 — T2: the register API
- **Endpoints** (`search.internal.decision`, tag `search`): `GET /api/v1/decision-register/scope` and `POST /api/v1/decision-register/search`, both `@SignedIn`, 403 without a register (former members, guests, item attendees, observers, alternates without a seat of their own, memo readers, action owners outside the committee). `DecisionRegister` calls T1's `DecisionRegisterAccess` once per request (scope, clearance and recusals computed in the request's transaction), checks the inputs, calls `find`, audits, maps rows (design §3 "as built").
- **Filters, orders, facets:** words (1–200 characters, 12 words; a number-like text looked up whole, otherwise the last plain word of 3+ letters as a prefix), committees (checked against the scope: `FORUM_OUT_OF_SCOPE`, a committee that does not exist too), committee types, meeting dates (25 years at most: `RANGE`), outcomes, resolutions only, states (with VOID), memo types, the five exception flags (every one must hold); RELEVANCE by default with words, NEWEST without, OLDEST, NUMBER (one committee: `SORT_NEEDS_ONE_FORUM`); totals to 10,000 with `capped`; facets per committee (with its name), outcome (FULL rows only) and year; `limit` 1–50.
- **Rows:** a LINE carries the committee, meeting, date, item number, state, resolution number and state, and the reason (RECUSED or RESTRICTED); never the title, outcome, text, conditions, memo, flags, `decidedAt` or the entry number. FULL rows add those, with the memo type's name.
- **Sealed cursors** (`RegisterCursors`): the keyset values as JSON, AES-256-GCM through `DataKeys`, with `tenant|viewer|fingerprint` as associated data (the fingerprint a SHA-256 over the normalised words, the sorted filters and the order). A cursor of another search, person or tenant, a changed byte or garbage is 422 `cursor INVALID`; a client cannot read a line's decision time from it.
- **Rate limit** `DECISION_SEARCH_PER_USER` (30 a minute, keyed by tenant and person, before the transaction). **Audit** `search.decisions.performed` (object `search`, the viewer): `textSha256`, `terms`, `numberLookup`, the filters (ids, dates, kinds), the order, `nextPage`, total, capped, the page's decision ids.
- **Published additions:** `ForumDirectory.all()` (forum) and `MemoSearchSource.allTypeNames()` (memo) for the scope.
- **Matrix** `p4-e08-register-analytics.yaml`: both endpoints, a user without a register 403, recusal columns 200 (each recused viewer is still in office; the rows are the leak suite's).
- **Leak suite:** `PaperSurfaceRegistry` gains the REGISTER surface and both endpoints; `modules` is `com.memo.search` and `com.memo.analytics` in place of `com.memo.decision`; the memo search and saved-search endpoints are listed in `search` with their reason; the E08 placeholder now covers analytics and the register's export only (T3, T4). `RecusalLeakIT`: the REGISTER probe in the shared scenario (scope, every canary searched: nothing found or counted); `RegisterLeakIT` (its own class: `RecusalLeakIT` is at detekt's size limit), on the same planted meeting, both planted items decided, numbered and indexed, canaries in the decisions' text and conditions and a dissent's reason: the recused member, the awaiting member, the recused secretary and the recused chair get each as a RECUSED line without content keys; words, "term loan", an outcome and the conditions and dissent flags find neither; outcome facets count FULL rows only; the number finds the line only; `forum.read` reads the shown item in full and the hidden one as a line; the observer, the guest and someone outside are 403; no canary or word in the audit viewer. `PaperLeakIT`: the planted decisions indexed, the REGISTER probe for d3, the guest, `forum.read`, d1 (recused) and d1's alternate: levels by the rule (FULL, LINE or absent above clearance), lines without content, no canary found; the guest and the alternate 403. `DecisionPlanting` takes `conditions`.
- **Tests:** `DecisionRegisterIT` (every filter alone and with others, words with a prefix, the number in three spellings and the memo's number, the orders and `SORT_NEEDS_ONE_FORUM`, totals and facets; pages by sealed cursors and five refused cursors; every 422 and the 405 of a GET; who has a register and the levels of a restricted, title-hidden item for each viewer, the scope's flags; a declaration applied at once for a member and the secretary, awaiting a ruling still a line, withdrawn back in full; the audit with the hash and no words; the 31st search 429).
