## 2026-11-22 — T2: the register API
- **Endpoints** (`search.internal.decision`, tag `search`): `GET /api/v1/decision-register/scope` and `POST /api/v1/decision-register/search`, both `@SignedIn`, 403 without a register (former members, guests, item attendees, observers, alternates without a seat of their own, memo readers, action owners outside the committee). `DecisionRegister` calls T1's `DecisionRegisterAccess` once per request (scope, clearance and recusals computed in the request's transaction), checks the inputs, calls `find`, audits, maps rows (design §3 "as built").
- **Filters, orders, facets:** words (1–200 characters, 12 words; a number-like text looked up whole, otherwise the last plain word of 3+ letters as a prefix), committees (checked against the scope: `FORUM_OUT_OF_SCOPE`, a committee that does not exist too), committee types, meeting dates (25 years at most: `RANGE`), outcomes, resolutions only, states (with VOID), memo types, the five exception flags (every one must hold); RELEVANCE by default with words, NEWEST without, OLDEST, NUMBER (one committee: `SORT_NEEDS_ONE_FORUM`); totals to 10,000 with `capped`; facets per committee (with its name), outcome (FULL rows only) and year; `limit` 1–50.

## 2026-11-22 — T4: analytics backend
- **The `analytics` module** (`com.memo.analytics`: `api`, `measures`, `export`; no table, no job). It depends on meeting, actions, search, forum, org, workflow (the business calendar), audit and common; nothing depends on it.
- **The measures as built** (`analytics.measures`, pure, one definition for the screen, the export and the tests; PIT targets them nightly):
  - **M1 attendance** (`AttendanceMeasure`): seat holders are the chair, vice-chairs and members in office on each held meeting's date (`ForumDirectory.membersOn`); `expected` = held meetings of their term; `attended` = their own register line reached PRESENT (first arrival, after voids; joining late, leaving early and stepping out of an item still attended); else APOLOGIES, else ABSENT (marked absent or never marked); `byAlternate` = an alternate's line for them arrived (shown, never counted); in person or remote by the first arrival's mode; the committee's rate is Σattended ÷ Σexpected; whole percents rounded half up.
  - **M2 actions** (`ActionMeasure`) over E07 T8's `ActionStats` (the one rule, the signed-in viewer): overdue now summed over owners with the bands 1–30, 31–90, over 90 and "re-dated"; closed in the period by the original and by the agreed date; owners most overdue first.
  - **M3 time to decision** (`DecisionMeasure.timing`): decisions on SUBMISSION items the viewer sees in FULL whose outcome is final (not DEFERRED or REFERRED), from the chain's first submission to the decision, as days in the tenant's zone: calendar days and working days (`WorkingDays.counter`, one calendar read for all spans); median and P90 by nearest rank; `enough` from 5 decisions; by quarter of the meeting date and by memo type.
  - **M4 items deferred** (`DecisionMeasure.deferrals`): of those decisions, the DEFERRED ones, and items deferred again (`prior_deferrals ≥ 1`) counted once per chain (memo, or submission, with the chain's start).
  - **M5 papers on time** (`TimelinessMeasure`): the first revision published on or before the papers deadline (in the meeting's zone); never published is late; no deadline (no working day to count) is left out of the share; late items and papers = LATE or TABLED rows of the latest revision the viewer sees in full, for officers and oversight only.
  - **Period** (`Period`): default the last 12 months to today in the tenant's zone; 422 `to RANGE` backwards, `from RANGE` above ten years; per-member rows need five years or less (the detail omits them above, `membersOmitted`; the export answers 422).
- **Published reads:**
  - meeting: `AttendanceStats.held(forumIds, from, to, clearanceRank)` (held meetings within the clearance, seats on the date, register lines; four queries), `AgendaTimeliness.of(meetingIds)` and `.lateCounts(meetingIds)` for the signed-in actor through the new `PaperAccess.late` (the paper rule with recusals; `forum.read` counts unrestricted items with their titles shown outside members-only sections, and their unrestricted papers); `AgendaDeadlines.of` added byte for byte as E07 T7 has it (one shared shape: whichever merges second merges cleanly);
  - search: `DecisionStats.of(forumIds, from, to)` over **`search/V5__decision_stats.sql`** (the next number after T1's V4): `search_decision_stats` dropped and created with the same arguments, returning one row per FULL decision on a SUBMISSION item (committee, memo type, memo, submission, meeting date, decided at, chain start, outcome, earlier deferrals), planned per call from `search_decision_stats_sql()`; at most ten years, 100,000 rows; the EXPLAIN gate covers it for the five profiles (summary over 12 months, detail over five years);
  - forum: `ForumDirectory.all()`; workflow: `WorkingDays.counter(from, to)`.
- **API:** `GET /api/v1/analytics/committees?from&to&forumIds` (one block per committee in scope, 422 `FORUM_OUT_OF_SCOPE`), `GET /api/v1/analytics/committees/{forumId}?from&to` (members per seat holder or the member's own row, owners with ageing, timing by quarter and memo type, deferrals by quarter, each held meeting's papers and attendance), `GET …/{forumId}/attendance/export` (XLSX for the annual report: header block, one row per seat holder; officers and oversight; `ANALYTICS_EXPORT_PER_USER` 5 an hour, counted after the 403; audited `analytics.attendance.exported` with the period and the row count before streaming). No scope → 403; a committee outside scope → 404. Reads are not audited (design §5).
- **Who sees what:** officers (secretary, chair, vice-chair in office), `forums.manage` and `forum.read` see everything (FULL); a member in office (MEMBER seat) sees their committees' totals, their own attendance row and the meeting-level share of papers on time; others 403. Held meetings above the viewer's clearance are left out of every measure. M3 and M4 use the register's own viewer parameters (`DecisionRegisterAccess`), so a vice-chair with a chair in office counts decisions under the members' rule there.
- **Tests:** unit `AttendanceMeasureTest`, `DecisionTimingTest`, `MeasuresTest` (actions, papers on time, periods, rounding); `AnalyticsIT` (viewer kinds, members' own row and no late counts, oversight, 403/404/422, periods, the export's sheet, formula prefix and audit, five an hour after the 403, a meeting above clearance left out, another tenant through the published reads and the service); `AnalyticsLeakIT` (**the differential**: the recused member's, the recused secretary's and `forum.read`'s item-level measures read after a first held meeting are unchanged after a second meeting whose only item they may not see in full is deferred, late and carries an overdue action; the chair, the control, counts all three); the leak suite's ANALYTICS surface in `PaperLeakIT` and `RecusalLeakIT` (no canary in any answer or workbook, members' own row, no late counts, members do not export); matrix `p4-e08-analytics.yaml`; `DecisionSearchExplainIT` gains the stats plans.
- **Deviations:**
