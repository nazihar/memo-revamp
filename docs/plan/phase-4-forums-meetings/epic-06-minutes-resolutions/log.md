- **Choices that came from the research:**
  - **The decision is a structured record** with named dissent (optional reason), named abstentions, an optional vote and "took no part" derived from declarations and attendance: CGI §5.3.3–5.3.4, Basel ¶60, and Bangladesh Bank's requirement that dissent be "comprehensively recorded".
  - **Resolution numbers at the meeting's end, in agenda order:** no voids for corrections made in the room; the period is the meeting's date.
  - **Memos move on when the meeting is marked as held by default**, with "when the minutes are approved" per committee type: s.249 makes minutes the evidence of a decision taken at the meeting; SS-1 allows certified extracts before the minutes are signed.
  - **Two approval modes per committee type:** at the next meeting (CGI §6.3: "virtually all organisations") or by circulation then noted (CGI §6.3's ratification; SS-1's comment window where silence is consent).
  - **Chair review before circulation** (CGI §6) and a comment box for members rather than co-editing (OnBoard users email revised versions; BoardPro keeps editing during review).
  - **Approved minutes are never edited:** corrections annexed in a new record version (CGI §6.2; BoardPro locks confirmed minutes).
  - **The secretary's notes are not part of the record** (CGI §8.1: notes are discoverable and usually destroyed after approval; here they are kept, never deleted, and visible only to secretaries).
- **Changes from the overview, recorded in ADR-0065 and ADR-0066, to report to the owner:**
  - numbering shares the format code (`common.numbering`), not one counter service: resolutions get their own counter, as meeting numbers already do, and no memo number or table changes (overview risk R8 avoided);
  - minutes approval follows a fixed path per committee type, not the workflow engine: the next-meeting step is another meeting's decision, the comment window is not a task, and each reader needs their own redacted copy, which the engine cannot give.
- **Found while reading:**
  - `ForumSubmissions.decide` is in `forum.internal`; T2 publishes `ForumDecisions` (decide, defer at a meeting, refer at a meeting).
  - `WorkflowForums.decided` takes only a remark; T2 adds `details` so the note sheet can link the resolution without a memo migration.
  - The PDF/A pipeline is internal to the memo module; T7 publishes `RecordPdfs` as `ReportPages` already is.
  - `templating` supports sections and table-row loops but not repeated blocks; T2 adds block loops (poi-tl's documented behaviour) without changing memo templates.
  - The meeting module cannot depend on minutes; E04's "declarations on a decided item change only by correction" goes through a `DecidedItems` port the minutes module implements, as E03's `PaperExclusions` hook did.
- **Uncertain until T1:** whether ONLYOFFICE 10.0.0.1 keeps a locked, tagged block-level content control through editing, undo and save.   - Word: the draft opened through COM automation, the deletions tried, a line typed in item 5, saved with personal information removed; kept as the golden file `templating/minutes-saved-by-word.docx` and checked in `ContentControlWrapperTest`.
- **ONLYOFFICE 10.0.0.1, edit mode:**
  - typing inside item 5's control and outside every control (the first line, the closing line) works; the edit stays inside the control;

  - select all, then Delete: **nothing is deleted**;

Inside, value tags, a nested `{{?item.declarations}}` section and an `{{item.votes}}` row loop resolve against the element, then against the document's values. Empty list: the block disappears. One level only. Tags or markup in values stay text.
  - Kept as code: `TemplateData.blocks` and `TemplateData.control` (`ControlMark`) in `templating`; the scanner and `TagVocabulary.blocks` are T2.

## 2026-11-21 — T4: resolution numbers and the way back to the memo
- **Migration `minutes/V2__resolutions.sql`:** `minutes_resolution_counter` (one per committee and period; `first_number` 1 or the starting number; `last_number` only grows, by trigger and column grant) and `minutes_resolution` (unique sequence per committee and period, unique number per tenant, one IN_FORCE per decision; the number never changes, a void is IN_FORCE → VOID once with its reason and entry). RLS ENABLE + FORCE, `tenant_id` first, composite foreign keys (to the decision and to the counter), no DELETE or TRUNCATE. jOOQ regenerated.
- **Numbering rules** (ADR-0065, ADR-0044): the counter taken with `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE … RETURNING` in the business transaction and locked to commit; the period and the rendering by the committee type's format pinned on the meeting (`BRC/2026/014`, yearly by default), the committee's code and the meeting's number; 409 `NUMBER_TAKEN` before anything commits.
  - at the meeting's end the `minutes-release` job numbers the current resolution decisions without a number in agenda order (`ResolutionOrder`: the latest revision, then tabled items; off the agenda last, by time), one transaction each, in rounds until none is left;
  - a decision recorded or corrected into a resolution once the meeting is held is numbered at once in its own transaction;
  - a correction to an outcome that is not a resolution voids the number (reason, entry); a decision that becomes a resolution again takes a new number; a void never frees a number;
  - the starting number, once per committee and period before any number, by `forums.manage` with a fresh step-up (`PUT /forums/{fid}/resolution-counters/{period}`; 422 `FORMAT` and `RANGE`, 409 `ALREADY_STARTED`);
  - the register check (`ResolutionChecks`, pure): every number from the first to the last once, in force or void; missing, duplicated and stray numbers listed.
- **The way back to the memo** (`DecisionRelease` with the pure `ReleaseMapping`, through T2's `ForumDecisions`): decided with the outcome (WITHDRAWN as RETURNED with "Withdrawn at the meeting."), deferred with its target date, referred with a linked submission, a pass-through noted; the remark "{label}. Resolution {number}: {text} Conditions: 1. …" cut at 4,000; the FORUM_DECIDED details carry the number, its id, the decision and the meeting. `released_at`/`released_kind` set once; audited `minutes.decision.released`.
  - **AT_MEETING_END** (default): `MeetingChanged(ENDED)` schedules the job, which releases every decision with a submission; a decision after the end is released at once. **ON_MINUTES_APPROVAL:** the end only numbers; `MinutesReleaseScheduler.approved(meetingId)` (T6 calls it at the confirmation) releases.
  - **T2's carried note:** `WorkflowForums` needs a signed-in actor, so the job releases each decision as its current entry's recorder (the audit says the trigger); the numbering is the job's system actor.

- **Lock order:** recording takes the decision row, the item's submission row, then the meeting row (`Meetings.locked`, published), so a meeting marked as held meanwhile numbers and releases at once; then the snapshot (same meeting row) and the counter. The job: the decision row, then the counter or the submission.
